Tips for iPhone

  • Open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen for full-screen mode.
  • Allow Location "While Using". Keep the screen on while recording a trail — iOS pauses web GPS when the screen locks. The app requests a screen wake lock while recording.
  • Long-press the map to drop a waypoint anywhere.
  • Give stands their good wind directions so forecasts can pick the right stand.
  • Export a backup now and then — data lives on this phone only.
